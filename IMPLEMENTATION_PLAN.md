## RISKS & MITIGATION

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Royal messaging too formal | Low engagement | Balance with personal stories |
| Content gaps | Incomplete site | Use template stories initially |
| Bilingual errors | Poor UX for Arabic users | Native speaker review |
| Design not "royal enough" | Doesn't stand out | Stakeholder feedback rounds |
| Too many pages = overwhelming | Navigation confusion | Strong information architecture |
