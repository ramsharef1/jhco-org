# JHCO Brand Voice Guide

## One-sentence summary

Formal institutional authority first, humanitarian mission second — every sentence
should sound like it was written by a royal institution that happens to do charity,
not a charity that happens to have royal backing.

## Person & register

- **Third person institutional** ("The Organization", "JHCO", never "I" or "we" in
  headlines; "we" is acceptable in body copy when addressing supporters directly).
- **Formal but not stiff.** Declarative sentences, active voice. No jargon. No
  exclamation marks. No rhetorical questions.
- **Arabic register:** Modern Standard Arabic (fusha). Never colloquial. Mirror the
  formality of the English — do not soften the Arabic version.

## Rhythm

- Lead with the credential, follow with the mission.  
  ✅ "Under Hashemite royal patronage since 1990, JHCO delivers…"  
  ❌ "JHCO is a charity that helps people…"
- Short opening sentences (under 15 words). Longer detail sentences follow.
- One idea per sentence. No compound-complex structures.

## Vocabulary

| Use                         | Avoid                        |
|-----------------------------|------------------------------|
| serve, deliver, provide     | help, give back, make a difference |
| governance, accountability  | trust us, believe in us      |
| measurable impact           | changing lives, touching hearts |
| professional, established   | passionate, heartfelt        |
| documented, verified        | incredible, amazing          |
| families, communities       | the poor, the needy, victims |
| programme participants      | beneficiaries (where possible) |

## What we never say

- Emotional appeals without evidence ("Every dollar saves a life").
- Superlatives without attribution ("The best charity in Jordan").
- Casual language ("Hey!", "Check this out!", "Don't miss!").
- Invented statistics — every number comes from `content/facts.ts`.
- First-person singular — no individual voice except in attributed quotes.

## Headlines

- Serif (Garamond/Georgia). Uppercase. 0.5px+ letter-spacing.
- State the fact, not the feeling.  
  ✅ "HEALTHCARE ACROSS 30 COUNTRIES"  
  ❌ "BRINGING HOPE TO MILLIONS"

## CTAs

- Imperative, formal, uppercase.  
  ✅ "VIEW REPORT" / "SUPPORT OUR WORK" / "EXPLORE PROGRAMS"  
  ❌ "Click here!" / "Join the movement!" / "Be the change!"

## Bilingual rules

- Arabic copy is a full localization, not a translation. It should read natively.
- Same factual claims in both languages — never add or remove information.
- RTL layout must mirror LTR exactly in structure.
- Gold borders switch from left to right in RTL.
