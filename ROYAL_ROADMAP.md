# 👑 JHCO Royal Charity Website - Complete Roadmap

## 🏛️ Project Overview

**Jordan Hashemite Charity Organization** - A royal charity under royal patronage representing the Jordanian royal family's humanitarian mission. Website featuring professional royal design with navy, gold, and crimson colors.

---

## 🎨 Design System

### **Royal Color Palette**
- **Royal Navy:** #0a1428 (primary)
- **24K Gold:** #d4af37 (accent)
- **Crimson:** #8b1a1a (secondary CTAs)
- **Ivory:** #f9f7f4 (backgrounds)

### **Design Elements**
- Heraldic borders (gold-crimson-gold gradient)
- Georgian serif typography (headings)
- Crown symbols (👑) and royal seals (⚜️)
- Professional luxury aesthetic
- Full bilingual (Arabic/English with RTL)
